========================================
CHAPTER 3: COLOURS
========================================

Colours are an important part of English vocabulary. The basic colours are: red,
blue, yellow, green, orange, purple, pink, brown, black, white, and grey.

Red is the colour of fire engines, roses, and blood. Blue is the colour of the sky
and the sea. Yellow is the colour of the sun and bananas. Green is the colour of
grass and trees. Orange is both a colour and a fruit. Purple is a mix of red and
blue. Pink is a light red colour. Brown is the colour of chocolate and mud.
Black is the darkest colour. White is the lightest colour. Grey is between black
and white.

Some colours have light and dark versions. Light blue is called sky blue or baby
blue. Dark blue is called navy blue. Light green can be called lime green.
Dark green is called forest green.

To describe something using a colour you put the colour before the noun: a red
apple, a blue sky, a yellow banana, a green leaf, a black cat, a white dog.
You can also say: "The apple is red." or "The sky is blue."
