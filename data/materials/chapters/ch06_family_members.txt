========================================
CHAPTER 6: FAMILY MEMBERS
========================================

A family is a group of people who are related to each other. Family members in
English: mother (mum/mom), father (dad), sister, brother, grandmother (grandma),
grandfather (grandpa), aunt, uncle, cousin, daughter, son, wife, husband, and baby.

A nuclear family usually means parents and their children. An extended family
includes grandparents, aunts, uncles, and cousins.

To describe family relationships you say: "She is my sister.", "He is my father.",
"They are my parents.", "She is my grandmother on my mother's side."

Possessive adjectives used with family: my mother, your father, his sister,
her brother, our grandparents, their children. The possessive 's is also common:
"This is Tom's sister." or "Anna's mum works in a hospital."

Common questions about family: "How many brothers and sisters do you have?",
"Do you have any siblings?", "What does your father do?", "Where does your
family live?"
