========================================
CHAPTER 9: WEATHER AND SEASONS
========================================

The weather tells us what the sky and air are like outside. Common weather words:
sunny, cloudy, windy, rainy, snowy, foggy, stormy, and icy. To ask about the
weather: "What is the weather like today?" or "How is the weather?"

Weather descriptions: "It is sunny today.", "It is raining.", "It is snowing.",
"It is cold and windy.", "It is warm and cloudy.", "There is a storm coming."

Temperature is measured in degrees Celsius (°C) in Finland and most of Europe,
and in degrees Fahrenheit (°F) in the United States. Water freezes at 0°C and
boils at 100°C. A warm summer day in Finland might be 25°C. A cold winter day
might be -15°C.

Season vocabulary:
Spring — warm, flowers bloom, birds return, rainy, green.
Summer — hot, sunny, long days, holidays, swimming, light nights.
Autumn — cool, leaves change colour (red, orange, yellow), windy, shorter days.
Winter — cold, snow, ice, dark, short days, skiing, Christmas.

Useful weather vocabulary: umbrella (used when it rains), raincoat, boots, sunglasses
(used when it is sunny), scarf, gloves, and hat (used when it is cold).
