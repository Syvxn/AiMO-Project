========================================
CHAPTER 8: ANIMALS
========================================

Animals are living creatures. They are divided into several groups. Mammals are
warm-blooded animals that feed their young with milk. Examples: dog, cat, horse,
cow, elephant, lion, dolphin, and whale. Birds have feathers and wings. Examples:
eagle, penguin, sparrow, robin, duck, owl, and parrot.

Reptiles have scales and are cold-blooded. Examples: snake, lizard, crocodile,
and turtle. Amphibians live on land and in water. Examples: frog and salamander.
Fish live in water and breathe through gills. Examples: salmon, tuna, goldfish,
and shark. Insects have six legs. Examples: butterfly, ant, bee, and mosquito.

Common pets: dog, cat, rabbit, hamster, guinea pig, goldfish, budgerigar (budgie),
and turtle. Dogs are often called man's best friend. Cats are independent and clean.

Wild animals found in Finland: elk (moose), reindeer, brown bear, wolf, fox,
lynx, beaver, otter, and many species of birds.

Animal sounds in English: a dog barks, a cat meows, a cow moos, a sheep bleats,
a horse neighs, a duck quacks, a lion roars, a bird sings/chirps.

To describe animals you can use adjectives: fast, slow, big, small, dangerous,
friendly, fluffy, scaly, and wild.

Animal habitats:
forest, ocean, river, desert, jungle, farm, and Arctic.
Example: Polar bears live in the Arctic.
Example: Camels live in the desert.

What animals eat:
Herbivores eat plants.
Carnivores eat meat.
Omnivores eat both plants and meat.

Body part vocabulary:
fur, feathers, wings, paws, claws, tail, beak, and fins.

Sentence practice:
"A dolphin is a mammal, but it lives in water."
"Owls are birds that can see well at night."

Animal movement words:
run, jump, swim, fly, crawl, climb, hop, and slither.
A frog hops. A snake slithers. An eagle flies.

Baby animal words:
puppy, kitten, calf, chick, cub, lamb, and foal.

Farm animals:
cow, pig, sheep, goat, horse, chicken, and duck.
Farm animals can give us milk, eggs, wool, or help with work.

More wild animals:
tiger, zebra, giraffe, monkey, kangaroo, panda, and polar bear.

Animal facts:
Giraffes have very long necks.
Penguins are birds, but they cannot fly.
Bats are mammals that can fly.
Bees help flowers by carrying pollen.

Protecting animals:
People should take care of pets.
Wild animals need safe habitats.
Pollution can harm animals in rivers and oceans.

Question practice:
"Which animal lays eggs?"
"Which animal is known as the king of the jungle?"
"What is the difference between a pet and a wild animal?"
