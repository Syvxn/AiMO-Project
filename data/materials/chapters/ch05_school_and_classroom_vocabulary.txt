========================================
CHAPTER 5: SCHOOL AND CLASSROOM VOCABULARY
========================================

The classroom is where students learn. In a classroom you can find: a board
(blackboard or whiteboard), a teacher's desk, student desks, chairs, a projector,
windows, and doors.

School subjects taught in Finnish 5th grade include: English, Finnish, mathematics
(maths), science, history, arts and crafts, physical education (PE), music, and
religion or ethics.

Classroom objects: a pencil, a pen, a rubber (eraser), a ruler, a pencil case,
a notebook, a textbook, a schoolbag (backpack), scissors, glue, and coloured
pencils.

Useful classroom phrases: "May I go to the toilet?", "Can you repeat that,
please?", "I don't understand.", "Can you help me?", "What does this word mean?",
"How do you spell that?", "Is this correct?", "Can I borrow a pencil?"

The teacher gives homework and tests. Students study, read, write, listen, and
speak in lessons. A lesson is usually forty-five minutes long. There are breaks
between lessons. Lunch break is the longest break in the school day.

People at school:
head teacher (principal), class teacher, subject teacher, student, classmate,
school nurse, and librarian.

Useful action verbs:
open your book, close your notebook, answer the question, work in pairs,
raise your hand, underline the word, and check your answer.

Classroom rules examples:
"Be on time."
"Listen when others speak."
"Keep your desk tidy."
"Do your homework."

Short practice:
"Where is your ruler?" "It is in my pencil case."
"Can I borrow your eraser?" "Yes, of course."

Places in a school building:
classroom, corridor, gym, library, canteen, playground, art room, music room,
computer room, office, and toilet.

More school objects:
marker, chalk, map, calendar, dictionary, worksheet, laptop, tablet, and clock.

Lesson language:
"Open your books on page ten."
"Write the answer in your notebook."
"Read the text carefully."
"Listen to the audio."
"Discuss with your partner."

Homework language:
do homework, revise for a test, finish an exercise, return a library book,
pack your schoolbag, remember your PE clothes.

Subjects and activities:
In maths students solve problems.
In English students read, write, speak, and listen.
In science students learn about plants, animals, weather, and space.
In PE students run, jump, stretch, and play games.

Example sentences:
"The teacher writes on the whiteboard."
"The students sit at their desks."
"We go to the library every Wednesday."
"Our class has music after lunch."

Question practice:
"Which subject do you like best?"
"Where do students eat lunch?"
"What do you keep in your schoolbag?"
