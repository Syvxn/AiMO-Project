========================================
CHAPTER 5: SCHOOL AND CLASSROOM VOCABULARY
========================================

The classroom is where students learn. In a classroom you can find: a board
(blackboard or whiteboard), a teacher's desk, student desks, chairs, a projector,
windows, and doors.

School subjects taught in Finnish 5th grade include: English, Finnish, mathematics
(maths), science, history, arts and crafts, physical education (PE), music, and
religion or ethics.

Classroom objects: a pencil, a pen, a rubber (eraser), a ruler, a pencil case,
a notebook, a textbook, a schoolbag (backpack), scissors, glue, and coloured
pencils.

Useful classroom phrases: "May I go to the toilet?", "Can you repeat that,
please?", "I don't understand.", "Can you help me?", "What does this word mean?",
"How do you spell that?", "Is this correct?", "Can I borrow a pencil?"

The teacher gives homework and tests. Students study, read, write, listen, and
speak in lessons. A lesson is usually forty-five minutes long. There are breaks
between lessons. Lunch break is the longest break in the school day.
