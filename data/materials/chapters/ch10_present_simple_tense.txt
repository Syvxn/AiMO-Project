========================================
CHAPTER 10: PRESENT SIMPLE TENSE
========================================

The present simple tense is used to talk about habits, routines, and facts that
are always true. It is one of the most important tenses in English.

Positive sentences: I eat breakfast every morning. She reads books. He plays
football on Saturdays. They go to school by bus. We live in Helsinki.

Negative sentences: use do not (don't) for I, you, we, they. Use does not
(doesn't) for he, she, it. I don't eat meat. She doesn't like coffee. They
don't watch TV on weekdays. He doesn't play video games.

Question form: use Do for I, you, we, they. Use Does for he, she, it.
"Do you like pizza?" "Does she speak French?" "Do they have a dog?"
"Does he play music?"

Short answers: "Yes, I do." / "No, I don't." "Yes, she does." / "No, she doesn't."

Third person singular (he, she, it) adds -s or -es to the verb:
play → plays, eat → eats, run → runs, watch → watches, go → goes, do → does.

Common time expressions used with present simple: every day, every week, always,
usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never, on Mondays, in the morning.

Example sentences with time expressions: "I always brush my teeth before bed.",
"She usually walks to school.", "He sometimes forgets his homework.",
"They never eat vegetables.", "We often play outside after school."

Verb form reminders:
I/You/We/They play.
He/She/It plays.
I/You/We/They do not like.
He/She/It does not like.

Spelling in third person singular:
study -> studies
watch -> watches
fix -> fixes
go -> goes

Question examples:
"Where do you live?"
"What time does school start?"
"Does your friend like football?"

Use present simple for facts:
"Water boils at one hundred degrees Celsius."
"The Earth goes around the Sun."

Daily routine examples:
"I wake up at seven o'clock."
"She brushes her teeth after breakfast."
"We start school at eight thirty."
"He does his homework in the evening."

Adverbs of frequency:
always, usually, often, sometimes, seldom, rarely, never.
These words tell us how often something happens.
"I usually drink milk with breakfast."
"They never arrive late."

Negative examples:
"I do not play tennis on Mondays."
"She does not like spicy food."
"We do not watch television before school."

Question and answer patterns:
"Do you walk to school?" "Yes, I do."
"Does he like reading?" "No, he does not."
"Do they live in a city?" "Yes, they do."

More spelling changes:
wash -> washes
pass -> passes
carry -> carries
try -> tries

Common uses of present simple:
habits, routines, timetables, instructions, and scientific facts.
"The train leaves at six."
"First, you open the book."

Practice prompts:
Write three sentences about your daily routine.
Write two questions about a friend's hobbies.
