========================================
CHAPTER 6: FAMILY MEMBERS
========================================

A family is a group of people who are related to each other. Family members in
English: mother (mum/mom), father (dad), sister, brother, grandmother (grandma),
grandfather (grandpa), aunt, uncle, cousin, daughter, son, wife, husband, and baby.

A nuclear family usually means parents and their children. An extended family
includes grandparents, aunts, uncles, and cousins.

To describe family relationships you say: "She is my sister.", "He is my father.",
"They are my parents.", "She is my grandmother on my mother's side."

Possessive adjectives used with family: my mother, your father, his sister,
her brother, our grandparents, their children. The possessive 's is also common:
"This is Tom's sister." or "Anna's mum works in a hospital."

Common questions about family: "How many brothers and sisters do you have?",
"Do you have any siblings?", "What does your father do?", "Where does your
family live?"

Describing family members:
"My mother is kind and patient."
"My brother is funny and energetic."
"My grandparents live in a small town."

Family activities:
cook dinner together, watch a film, go for a walk, visit relatives,
play board games, celebrate birthdays.

Examples with have/has:
"I have one sister."
"He has two cousins in Sweden."
"We have a big family dinner on Sundays."

Question practice:
"Who is the oldest person in your family?"
"What do you like to do with your family?"

More family words:
stepmother, stepfather, stepsister, stepbrother, niece, nephew, twins, and relatives.

Talking about age and jobs:
"My father is forty-two years old."
"My aunt is a doctor."
"My uncle works in a shop."
"My grandmother likes gardening."

Household roles and chores:
cook, clean, wash dishes, vacuum, set the table, water the plants, and walk the dog.

Family celebrations:
birthday parties, weddings, family dinners, Christmas, New Year, and summer trips.

Sentence patterns:
"There are five people in my family."
"I live with my mother, father, and little brother."
"My cousins visit us in the summer."
"Our family likes to travel together."

Questions for speaking:
"Do you share a room with anyone?"
"How often do you see your grandparents?"
"What is your family's favourite weekend activity?"

Reading example:
"Emma has a big family. She has two brothers and one sister. Her grandparents live near her home. On Sundays, the family often eats lunch together."
