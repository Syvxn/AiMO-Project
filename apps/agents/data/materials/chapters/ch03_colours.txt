========================================
CHAPTER 3: COLOURS
========================================

Colours are an important part of English vocabulary. The basic colours are: red,
blue, yellow, green, orange, purple, pink, brown, black, white, and grey.

Red is the colour of fire engines, roses, and blood. Blue is the colour of the sky
and the sea. Yellow is the colour of the sun and bananas. Green is the colour of
grass and trees. Orange is both a colour and a fruit. Purple is a mix of red and
blue. Pink is a light red colour. Brown is the colour of chocolate and mud.
Black is the darkest colour. White is the lightest colour. Grey is between black
and white.

Some colours have light and dark versions. Light blue is called sky blue or baby
blue. Dark blue is called navy blue. Light green can be called lime green.
Dark green is called forest green.

To describe something using a colour you put the colour before the noun: a red
apple, a blue sky, a yellow banana, a green leaf, a black cat, a white dog.
You can also say: "The apple is red." or "The sky is blue."

More colour words:
gold, silver, beige, turquoise, violet, maroon, and cream.

Colour combinations:
red + yellow = orange
blue + yellow = green
red + blue = purple
black + white = grey

Using colours in sentences:
"My favourite colour is green."
"She is wearing a pink jacket and black shoes."
"The Finnish flag is blue and white."
"We painted the wall light yellow."

Question practice:
"What colour is your schoolbag?"
"Do you like bright colours or dark colours?"

Bright and dark shades:
Bright colours are strong and easy to notice.
Pastel colours are soft and light.
Neon colours are very bright.

Colours in nature:
The grass is green.
Snow is white.
Coal is black.
Clouds can be white or grey.
Leaves can be green, yellow, orange, red, or brown.

Colours in clothes:
a red T-shirt, blue jeans, black boots, a white hat, a grey sweater.
People often talk about what someone is wearing by naming colours.

Favourite colours:
Different people like different colours.
Some people like warm colours such as red, orange, and yellow.
Some people like cool colours such as blue, green, and purple.

Warm and cool colours:
Warm colours can remind us of fire and the sun.
Cool colours can remind us of water, the sky, and ice.

Colour questions and answers:
"What colour are strawberries?" "They are red."
"What colour is milk?" "It is white."
"What colour are school buses in many countries?" "They are yellow."

Describing pictures:
"There is a brown dog on the green grass."
"The girl has a purple umbrella."
"The room has white walls and a blue carpet."

Colour words in art:
paint, draw, mix, shade, pattern, rainbow.
The rainbow has many colours, including red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and violet.
