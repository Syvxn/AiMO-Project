========================================
CHAPTER 9: WEATHER AND SEASONS
========================================

The weather tells us what the sky and air are like outside. Common weather words:
sunny, cloudy, windy, rainy, snowy, foggy, stormy, and icy. To ask about the
weather: "What is the weather like today?" or "How is the weather?"

Weather descriptions: "It is sunny today.", "It is raining.", "It is snowing.",
"It is cold and windy.", "It is warm and cloudy.", "There is a storm coming."

Temperature is measured in degrees Celsius (°C) in Finland and most of Europe,
and in degrees Fahrenheit (°F) in the United States. Water freezes at 0°C and
boils at 100°C. A warm summer day in Finland might be 25°C. A cold winter day
might be -15°C.

Season vocabulary:
Spring — warm, flowers bloom, birds return, rainy, green.
Summer — hot, sunny, long days, holidays, swimming, light nights.
Autumn — cool, leaves change colour (red, orange, yellow), windy, shorter days.
Winter — cold, snow, ice, dark, short days, skiing, Christmas.

Useful weather vocabulary: umbrella (used when it rains), raincoat, boots, sunglasses
(used when it is sunny), scarf, gloves, and hat (used when it is cold).

Weather forecasts:
"Tomorrow will be cloudy with light rain."
"There may be snow in the evening."
"The temperature will be around five degrees."

Extreme weather words:
thunder, lightning, blizzard, heatwave, flood, and drought.

Comparing weather:
"Today is colder than yesterday."
"July is usually warmer than April."
"Autumn is wetter than summer in many places."

Practical questions:
"Should I take an umbrella?"
"Do I need a warm jacket today?"

More weather verbs:
rain, snow, blow, shine, freeze, melt, and thunder.
"The wind blows strongly in autumn."
"Ice melts when the weather gets warm."

Clothing for the weather:
In hot weather people wear T-shirts, shorts, and caps.
In cold weather people wear coats, boots, scarves, gloves, and woollen hats.
In rainy weather people wear raincoats and waterproof boots.

Season activities:
In spring people plant flowers and ride bicycles.
In summer people swim, travel, and play outside.
In autumn people collect berries and watch leaves fall.
In winter people ski, skate, and build snowmen.

Weather safety:
Stay inside during a thunderstorm.
Drink water during a heatwave.
Walk carefully on icy roads.

Example forecast text:
"On Monday it will be windy and cloudy. On Tuesday it will be sunny but cool. On Wednesday there may be rain in the afternoon."

Question practice:
"What weather do you like best?"
"Which season is best for swimming?"
"What happens to water when it freezes?"
