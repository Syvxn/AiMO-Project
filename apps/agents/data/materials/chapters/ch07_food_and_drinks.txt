========================================
CHAPTER 7: FOOD AND DRINKS
========================================

Food vocabulary is very useful in everyday life. Common foods: bread, butter,
cheese, milk, eggs, rice, pasta, potatoes, chicken, fish, beef, pork, soup,
salad, pizza, sandwich, and cereal.

Fruits: apple, banana, orange, strawberry, blueberry, grapes, pear, lemon,
mango, and pineapple. Vegetables: carrot, onion, potato, tomato, cucumber,
broccoli, peas, corn, garlic, and cabbage.

Drinks: water, juice, milk, tea, hot chocolate, lemonade, and smoothie.
Children usually drink water, milk, or juice with meals.

At a restaurant or café you can say: "Can I have a glass of water, please?",
"I would like a cheese sandwich.", "What do you recommend?", "The bill, please."

Meal times: breakfast is in the morning, lunch is at midday, dinner (or supper)
is in the evening. A snack is a small amount of food eaten between meals.

To say what you like: "I love pizza.", "I like apples.", "I don't like onions.",
"I hate broccoli." To say what you are eating: "I am eating a banana.",
"She is drinking milk."

Healthy eating words:
vitamins, protein, carbohydrates, and fiber.
Fruit and vegetables are healthy choices.
Too much sugar is unhealthy.

Common meal examples:
Breakfast: porridge, bread, yogurt, fruit.
Lunch: soup, salad, sandwich, water.
Dinner: fish, rice, vegetables, milk.

At the table:
fork, knife, spoon, plate, bowl, cup, and napkin.

Useful requests:
"Can I have some more water, please?"
"Could you pass the bread, please?"
"This tastes delicious."

Food groups:
fruit, vegetables, grains, dairy, protein foods, and healthy fats.
Rice and bread are grains. Cheese and yogurt are dairy products.
Chicken, beans, eggs, and fish are protein foods.

Taste words:
sweet, sour, salty, bitter, spicy, and mild.
Chocolate is sweet. Lemons are sour. Crisps are salty.

Cooking verbs:
eat, drink, cook, bake, boil, fry, cut, mix, wash, peel, and serve.

Kitchen items:
fridge, oven, pan, pot, chopping board, bottle, and lunch box.

At the supermarket:
Customers buy food from shelves, baskets, and fridges.
They may buy a loaf of bread, a carton of milk, a bottle of juice, or a bag of rice.

Sentence examples:
"We eat breakfast before school."
"My favourite snack is a banana and yogurt."
"She drinks tea, but he drinks hot chocolate."
"Please wash the apple before you eat it."

Food quantity words:
some, any, much, many, a little, and a lot of.
"There is some juice in the fridge."
"Are there any apples on the table?"
"We have a lot of carrots at home."

Question practice:
"What do you usually eat for breakfast?"
"Which drink is healthier: water or fizzy drink?"
"What food do you take on a picnic?"
