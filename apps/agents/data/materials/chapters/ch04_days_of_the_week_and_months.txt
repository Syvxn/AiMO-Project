========================================
CHAPTER 4: DAYS OF THE WEEK AND MONTHS
========================================

There are seven days in a week. The days of the week in English are: Monday,
Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, and Sunday. Monday is the first
day of the school week. Saturday and Sunday are the weekend.

Each day has a short form: Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri, Sat, Sun.

There are twelve months in a year. The months are: January, February, March,
April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November, and December.
January is the first month. December is the last month.

Seasons of the year: spring (March–May), summer (June–August), autumn or fall
(September–November), and winter (December–February). In Finland, winter is cold
and snowy. Summer is warm and sunny.

To say what day it is: "Today is Monday." To ask what day it is: "What day is it
today?" To say what month it is: "It is April." To ask what the date is:
"What is the date today?"

Common prepositions with time: on Monday, on Tuesday, in January, in summer,
at noon, at midnight, at three o'clock, in the morning, in the afternoon,
in the evening, at night.

Talking about schedules:
"I have maths on Tuesday."
"We visit grandma on Sunday."
"Our summer holiday starts in June."
"School ends in May."

Date format examples:
British style: 22 April 2026
Common spoken form: the twenty-second of April, twenty twenty-six

Monthly events:
January: New Year
April: spring weather and longer days
June: school holiday begins
December: winter celebrations

Practice questions:
"What day comes after Wednesday?"
"Which month has your birthday?"

School week examples:
Many children go to school from Monday to Friday.
They rest or do hobbies on Saturday and Sunday.
Some clubs meet after school on weekdays.

Remembering the months:
January, February, March, April, May, June,
July, August, September, October, November, December.

Month facts:
February is the shortest month.
July is often one of the warmest months in Finland.
December is often cold and dark.
August is a common back-to-school month.

Talking about birthdays and holidays:
"My birthday is in September."
"Christmas is in December."
"New Year's Day is in January."
"Midsummer is in June."

Useful date questions:
"What is the date today?"
"When is your birthday?"
"Which month comes before April?"
"Which day comes before Friday?"

Prepositions review:
Use on with days: on Monday, on Friday evening.
Use in with months and seasons: in October, in winter.
Use at with clock times: at seven o'clock, at noon.

Example timetable sentences:
"We have English on Thursday morning."
"The school trip is in May."
"Football practice is on Wednesday at five o'clock."

Sequencing words:
first, next, then, after that, finally.
Example: "First comes Monday, then Tuesday, then Wednesday."
